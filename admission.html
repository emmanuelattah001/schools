Admission Requirements

Undergraduate Programs +
  • Completed application form
  • Minimum of 5 credits in relevant subjects including English and Mathematics (not more than two sittings)
  • Birth certificate or proof of age
  • One recent passport photograph
  • Application fee (non-refundable)
Postgraduate Programs +
  • Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field from a recognized institution
  • Minimum CGPA of 2.50 on a 5.00 scale (or equivalent)
  • Academic transcripts
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Statement of purpose (500–700 words)
  • Application fee (non-refundable)
International Students +
  • Proof of English language proficiency (TOEFL, IELTS, or equivalent)
  • Copy of international passport (bio-data page)
  • Student visa requirements as stated by immigration authority
  • Health clearance report
Special Requirements & Notes +
  • Equivalency and credential checks may be required for certain qualifications.
  • Some programs may request a portfolio, audition, or entrance test.
  • All applicants must meet the university’s code of conduct and eligibility criteria.
